# Project workflow

- Maintain PROMPTS.md: append each new user development prompt verbatim, excluding credentials and private access tokens. Clearly label summaries and omissions.
- Record actual outcomes and tests; never fabricate student reflections or claim unperformed verification.
- Keep RUN_GAME.md aligned with the implementation. It remains a draft until its instructions work from a fresh copy.
- Keep source and submission documentation in Git. Do not commit secrets, local credentials, generated caches, or large video recordings.
- Preserve the assignment requirements: 3D gameplay, player-to-NPC dialogue and directions, and NPC-to-NPC interactions.
- Teaching preference: explain each meaningful feature in plain language, identify the relevant code, and give the student a concrete observation or small experiment. Preserve their role in design and testing; do not write reflections on their behalf.
