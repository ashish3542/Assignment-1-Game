# Assignment submission checklist

- [ ] Finish and test the 3D game.
- [ ] Demonstrate talking to NPCs.
- [ ] Demonstrate directing NPCs.
- [ ] Demonstrate NPCs interacting with one another.
- [ ] Connect GitHub, create a repository, and push the source and documentation.
- [ ] Verify the instructor can access the repository link.
- [ ] Upload PROMPTS.md as the prompt record (Markdown or TXT required).
- [ ] Finish and verify RUN_GAME.md, then upload it (Markdown or TXT required).
- [ ] Record and upload a video demonstrating the working game.

## Suggested video sequence

Show the game starting, move through the world, talk to an NPC, assign a task, show two NPCs cooperating, and demonstrate the result. Briefly explain one genuine iteration and what you learned. Confirm any video length or file-format requirements in Canvas before recording.

## Learning notes

Record the student's own observations as development proceeds: what was requested, what worked, what failed, how it was refined, and what was learned. These notes must reflect actual experience.
